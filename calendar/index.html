Instructions for sellers

Our scout troop acquires funds by selling the scouts' advent calendars. The sale of advent calendars is absolutely the most important form of fundraising for our scout troop.

Lue sivu suomeksi

Calendar distribution

Calendars will be distributed during the normal scout night on Mon 19.10. Our goal is to acquire funds that will allow us to hold free spring and fall trips next year as well. In addition to this, we procure common equipment for the scout troop, such as tents and hiking equipment. During the year, we participate in numerous events in different age groups!

We ask you to participate in joint fundraising and we have set the goal of selling 12 calendars per scout. The scout is the best seller of the advent calendar. Parents can gladly advertise the calendars to their colleagues and acquaintances and help the child in this way. More calendars can be obtained from the group leaders on Monday evenings.

Last year, Espoon Leppäversot (our troop) sold 506 calendars. Great job! Although selling advent calendars for the common good teaches scouts important skills, the sales situation itself can be exciting, especially the first few times.

Sales

Scouts are identified by their scarves, and scarves should always be worn when selling advent calendars. If the calendars are sold outside, it is also important to dress warmly.

The youngest scouts sell advent calendars in pairs or groups. Of course, you can also tour a familiar neighbourhood alone. In door-to-door sales, safety is important:

  • It's good to respect people's evening peace.
  • Sellers always move in pairs or in a larger group.

Cash and MobilePay

It is good to give instructions on how to handle money. It would be good for the seller to know at least how to give back the correct change. MobilePay payment is also possible this year.

MobilePay payments are automatically sent to the scout troop's bank account. The selling price of the calendar is 13 € (notice the price increase from last year), of which our scout troop receives 5.59 €. The rest of the money goes to the Scouts of the Capital Region and the Scouts of Finland. This money is used to finance larger camps, for example.

Returning unsold calendars

We understand that sales don't always go through, no matter how fine-tuned your sales strategies and how good your spirit. In addition, other scout troops are also selling calendars, so you should be on time. Unsold calendars must be returned to the scout meeting no later than Monday 30 November. It's not possible to return calendars at the Christmas party on 7 December anymore.

It is absolutely essential that the unsold calendars are returned so that we can return them back. Otherwise, we will have to pay for each unsold calendar. Additional instructions can be asked from the group leaders.
